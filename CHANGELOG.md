# Hussons One release notes

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-04

- Introduced the Hussons One product identity and multilingual AZ/RU/EN interface.
- Added isolated company workspaces, branches, warehouses, memberships and role-based access.
- Added retail and electronics catalogues with variants, barcode search, IMEI/serial tracking and warranty data.
- Added purchasing, stock movements, POS sales, payments, internal receipts, discounts and inspected returns.
- Added pharmacy batches, expiry alerts, FEFO allocation and prescription controls.
- Added restaurant tables, kitchen workflow, order settlement and stock-backed checkout.
- Added brands, suppliers, customers, team invitations, platform administration and append-only audit records.
- Added operation idempotency, database-level identifier uniqueness and SQLite/MySQL coverage.

External Azerbaijani fiscalization remains disabled until a verified provider adapter is configured.
